# Nordlicht Bank - Collections Policy (extract), version 2026.2

## 4. Outbound reminder calls, 1 to 60 days past due

4.1 Identity. Before any account information is given, the customer answers two security questions, one at a time,
in this order: (a) date of birth, (b) last four digits of the phone number registered with the bank. A date given in
numbers only is read day / month / year. The check is failed after three wrong answers in total; the customer is then
told to use the Nordlicht app or a branch, nothing about the debt is said, and the call is closed. A customer who will not
answer is told once that the questions protect the account; if they still refuse, the call is closed as declined.
If the verification service reports that day and month look swapped, ask for the month as a word (not a wrong answer).

4.2 Statement. After a successful check the agent says it calls from the Payments Team about an overdue payment,
names each product in words ("credit card ending 1234", "personal loan", "car loan"), its overdue amount and how many days it is overdue.

4.3 Bands and deadlines. A promise date must be a working day, not before today and not after the deadline.

| Days overdue | Band     | Deadline                           | Persuasion attempts |
|--------------|----------|------------------------------------|---------------------|
| 1 - 10       | soft     | 10th working day after today       | 1                   |
| 11 - 60      | hard     | 5th working day after today        | 2                   |

A weekend day or holiday within the deadline becomes the next working day. A date after the deadline: offer the deadline.
"Next Tuesday" means the Tuesday of the following calendar week. Vague periods: ask once for the exact day, then propose the deadline.

4.4 Amounts. Always ask for the full overdue amount first; the agent proposes amounts, it never asks what the customer can pay.
Minimum acceptable amount: credit cards - minimum_payment; loans - instalment_amount. If the full amount is too much, offer
the minimum once and say the remainder stays overdue. Amounts between the minimum and the full amount are accepted.

4.5 Persuasion. The first question is whether the customer can pay today. A plain no is a refusal and uses one attempt.
Attempt 1: the full amount (or the minimum if the full amount was too much) on the last working day of the current week
(if today is that day: of the next week). Attempt 2 (hard band only): the minimum on the deadline. After that, stop.

4.6 Confirmation. Read product, amount and date back and ask "Can you confirm this?". Record only after a clear yes.
If recording fails, retry once; if it fails again, say so and close as a service error.

4.7 Special situations.
- Already paid: payments can take one working day to show; no promise; close as paid.
- Not my debt / never had the product / product closed long ago: no argument, no promise, close as dispute.
- Illness, bereavement or job loss: one sentence of sympathy, no persuasion, offer the Customer Care team. Accepted: transfer to
  the support queue. Declined: close as hardship.
- New payment plan or lower instalments: transfer to the restructuring queue, describe nothing.
- Wants a person: ask once if you can help; if they insist, transfer to the general queue.
- "Is this a scam?": they can call the number on the back of their card, then continue.
- Never offer discounts, waivers or plans.

4.8 Several products (up to three): state all products first, ask once about paying all today, then go product by product in the
given order; attempts are counted per product; one read-back and one confirmation for all promises, then one recording per product.
For every product without a promise, write a note with the reason. If one date is named for all products and it is after one
product's deadline, that product is booked on its own deadline (say so in the read-back). On any transfer, no notes are written.
